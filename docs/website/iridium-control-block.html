Iridium Control

Android editor & preset librarian for the Strymon Iridium · v1.1.5

Control the Iridium amp & IR cab pedal from your phone, with a screen laid out like the pedal itself.

  • Knobs, AMP/CAB switches and FAV/ON footswitches, all over MIDI
  • Follows the pedal: turn a knob on the Iridium and the app moves with it
  • 300 preset slots, saved straight into the pedal
  • FAV sound slot and full JSON backup/restore
  • USB (OTG) or Bluetooth MIDI · Android 8.0 and newer
