Edit the Strymon Iridium over USB MIDI – knobs, amps, cabs, live settings and a 300-slot preset library. Also as an Android app.
This browser can't talk to MIDI devices. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, or Chrome on Android with a USB OTG cable. You can still plan presets here and export them to a file.
